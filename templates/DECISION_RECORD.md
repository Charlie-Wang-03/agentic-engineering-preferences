# Decision Record

## Context

What decision is being made, and what project outcome matters?

## Default

Which methodology principle or existing project default applies?

## Conflict

What real constraint makes the default insufficient or costly here?

## Trade-off

Compare the meaningful consequences of the viable options. Consider only dimensions relevant to this decision.

## Decision / Exception

What will be done? If this deviates from a default, keep the exception as narrow as practical.

## Evidence

What evidence supports the decision? Examples: tests, benchmarks, user needs, issue discussion, operational constraints, or maintenance experience.

## Risks and Reversibility

What can go wrong? How difficult is rollback or replacement?

## Revisit Condition

What change in assumptions, tools, costs, evidence, or scope should trigger reevaluation?
